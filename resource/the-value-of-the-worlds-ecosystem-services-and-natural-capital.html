The Value of the World's Ecosystem Services and Natural Capital

Robert Costanza, Ralph d'Arge, Rudolf de Groot, Stephen Farber, Monica Grasso, Bruce Hannon, Karin Limburg, Shahid Naeem, Robert V. O'Neill, Jose Paruelo, Robert G. Raskin, Paul Sutton, Marjan van den Belt1997

Climate & Environment
Read at source Cite

Summary

Seminal Nature paper estimating the value of 17 ecosystem services for 16 biomes at an average of $33 trillion per year (1997 values), demonstrating that ecosystem services are critical to human welfare and substantially exceed global GNP.
