About Development Discourses

Development Discourses is a curated library from ImpactMojo, a platform for free development economics and policy education in South Asia.

The collection brings together research papers, books and grey literature on the themes that matter to development practitioners, from monitoring and evaluation to climate resilience, from gender justice to public health, and from data governance to livelihoods.

We put open access resources first, because they are free to read, cite and share. Each resource carries one of three access labels: open access, free to read or check access. We believe knowledge should not sit behind paywalls, especially knowledge about building a more equitable world.
